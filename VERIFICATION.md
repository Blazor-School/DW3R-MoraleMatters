## 1.9.0 guard pressure and local breakthroughs — 5 November 2026

### Implementation

- New `GuardPressureModel.h` / `GuardPressure.inc` count blocked player melee contacts per defender. Native `EAttackFlag::Charge` (`1`) supplies charge pressure; this does not infer charge attacks from damage, combo length or input buttons.
- The interior detour at `0x1397DF6` runs after native guard eligibility is calculated. It preserves the native frame alignment, volatile registers and flags, and requests the existing guard-break branch through its local break decision. Native motion `0x31` and component guard/break flags (`+0x141/+0x142`) select the game's reaction and effect. No shared attack data is modified and no damage is added.
- Active attack/charge/heavy-attack tags are excluded, preserving the subsequent native weapon-clash veto and glowing counterattack interaction. Mounted contacts, Musou, projectiles, throws, fixed damage, unblockables, native guard-break immunity, players, allies and script-protected enemies retain native handling.
- Defaults: normal/charge pressure `1/3`; ordinary/elite/named-officer thresholds `6/9/12`; recovery delay `2 s`; base recovery `2 pressure/s`; guard opening `0.9 s`; extra pressure-break cooldown `3 s`. High morale improves delayed pressure recovery, without raising thresholds. The opening suppresses subsequent eligible guard decisions without repeatedly restarting the break reaction. Native guaranteed breaks remain available during cooldown.
- A successful break can disrupt the nearest `6` eligible ordinary melee troops within `4 m` in three dimensions. Base hesitation is `0.8 s`, scaled to `1.2 s` at very low morale and `0.52 s` at very high morale. Source-group and target cooldowns bound repeated pulses. Active attacks, named officers, stopped AI and protected/scripted orders are respected. Local decision context is restored after the native tick; genuine native order changes win. This does not inflict morale loss, extra HP damage or cascading breaks.
- Per-actor state is bounded, identity-checked and reset with battle state. Durations advance with native battle callbacks, not wall-clock polling. Both supported executable layouts match the new byte probes.
- All `15` settings have UI properties, controls, validation, invariant-culture INI serialization, legacy-INI defaults and packaged-ASI version recognition.

### Implemented behavior

- Defaults: Reinforcements.Enabled=1, MoralePerTenPercent=10, WaveMoraleCap=50, WaveSeconds=3. Gain/cap accept 0-898; the quiet window accepts 0.25-10 battle seconds.
- A previously reserved native reinforcement group can qualify only once per battle. Existing groups, dead-group reactivation, healing, actor visibility and ordinary corps/side transfers do not generate arrivals. Entirely new corps with zero soldiers before the wave get no additional starting morale.
- Soldiers exclude each group's leader. Groups arriving within the quiet window share the pre-wave soldier denominator and one cap. Native activation must complete before the reward; stuck requests expire after 30 battle seconds without reward.
- Target is min(cap, gain-per-ten-percent * 10 * arriving / previous). The missing portion is added permanently to native base morale, preserving the support bonus ledger, expiry, event/casualty changes and 899 ceiling.
- Recent positive native changes and positive native changes during the wave count toward the target. This attribution is deliberately conservative: unrelated native gains nearby in battle time may reduce the top-up. Linked scenario-event credit is additionally retained beyond the quiet window, without double-counting the same native gain in both ledgers. Late same-event native awards replace previously paid mod gains, limited to that wave's remaining paid amount. Larger native rewards are retained. Multiple event keys can share one capped wave; one event can span several waves.
- The no-wave path does no additional group scan. Open waves scan validated native formation storage; this avoids introducing reflected character walks or per-group VirtualQuery calls into the normal polling path.
